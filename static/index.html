Key Viva Defense Line: "Gaussian MLE maximizes ∏ N(xi; μ, σ2). The MLE variance is mathematically biased because the true mean μ is replaced with sample estimate x̄, costing 1 degree of freedom."

Member 4: Transparent Limitations & Viva Defense

  • Bootstrap Data: Public receipt/form corpora prove the mathematics and code; real clinical relationships require approved hospital access.
  • Gated Corpora: MIMIC-III and n2c2 require signed Data Use Agreements, which is why STG rules are implemented via symbolic knowledge engineering.
  • Image Statistics vs LayoutLMv3: Classical whole-image features achieve ~91% accuracy; multimodal transformers (LayoutLMv3) are proposed for dense hospital records.
  • Targets are Design Goals: Poster metrics (90% Acc, 8% CER) represent operational specifications, not claims of clinical deployment.
